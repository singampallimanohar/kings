Our Story & Passion

Where Royal Flavours Meet
Warm Family Hospitality

Kings Biryani's and Family Restaurant is Tuni's cherished destination for authentic slow-dum biryani and memorable family meals.

Crafted with traditional culinary pride, our biryanis are prepared in sealed handis using aged long-grain basmati rice, farm-fresh tender meat, and crushed whole spices. From our peaceful air-conditioned dining hall to quick takeaway parcels, we treat every guest with genuine warmth.
