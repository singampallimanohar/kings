Our Culinary Mission

The True Essence of
Authentic Dum Cooking

Kings Biryani's and Family Restaurant was established in Tuni with a clear ambition: to serve royal, authentic, slow-cooked dum biryani alongside mouth-watering regional specialties in a clean, hospitable, family-friendly environment.

Every morning, our kitchen begins with the careful selection of premium aged basmati rice, farm-fresh tender poultry and mutton, whole royal spices (shahi jeera, green cardamom, star anise, mace, cinnamon), pure desi ghee, and fresh garden mint and coriander.

Our master cooks follow the time-honored slow-dum technique: sealing the large cooking deg/handi with dough so the fragrant aromas, delicate steam, and natural meat juices circulate within the vessel, infusing every single grain of basmati with extraordinary flavor.

Kitchen Standards

Our Three Guiding Promises

From sourcing to serving, we adhere to the highest standards of culinary hygiene and hospitality.

1. Traditional Dum Cooking

We do not take shortcuts. We cook in small authentic dum batches throughout lunch and dinner hours so that every patron receives fresh, piping hot rice with tender meat.

2. Pure & Wholesome Spices

Our masalas are prepared in-house by crushing whole spices. We use genuine saffron strands, aged ginger-garlic paste, and pure curd to create delicate and digestion-friendly gravies.

3. Genuine Hospitality

We believe that good food tastes even better with courteous service. Our staff is trained to accommodate families with children, senior citizens, and travelers passing through Tuni.
